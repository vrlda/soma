# SOMA: a synthetic brain

SOMA is a research project to build an AI from a synthetic brain: neurons
and synapses that hold numbers, change those numbers by local rules with
every experience, grow and prune their own connections and cells, and sleep
to consolidate what they learned. It is not a Transformer and not trained by
backpropagation. It learns continuously, for its whole life.

It is a dream we are researching, aiming at a breakthrough, not a product.
