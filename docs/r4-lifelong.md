conflict, a correction, interference, and return: uptake 4/4, latest-wins
conflict with logged record, correction takes effect, 3/4 retained, E0

(old/new source, resolution); removal stays exact via unobserve/entry
deletion. Consolidation runs through rehearsal into turn-scoped tables
